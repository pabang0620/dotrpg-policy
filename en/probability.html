Dungeon Reward Cards: Unique and Legendary (not paid currency)

Each of the 4 clear reward cards of weekday and normal dungeons (sure material, sure gold and 2 luck cards) turns into a Legendary or Unique piece of that difficulty's gear tier at the rates below. Gear rolled on a card is Epic at most. A Unique first picks evenly among the slots where you have no Unique of that tier yet; within a slot each piece is equally likely. Weapons are always of your equipped weapon type (with no weapon, the default type of your career). A tier with no Legendary gear shows 0%. Normal dungeons (no entry limit) give no Legendary at any difficulty and no Unique on Nightmare, Inferno and Abyss (the table shows weekday dungeons). Sweeps and raids do not use this.

Daily and Weekly Completion Chests (rate table version dailychest-3, not paid currency)

Finishing all of a day's daily jobs opens one daily chest; finishing all weekly jobs opens one weekly chest. The daily chest steps up with consecutive days opened and reaches the top step every 7 days; missing a day starts again from step 1 (yesterday's jobs can still be finished today).
