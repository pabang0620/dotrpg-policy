Deleting Your Account

This page explains how to delete your Bolon account and data, operated by 네코랩 (nekolab).

1. Deleting in the game

  1. Start the game and log in online.
  2. On the character selection screen, choose 회원 탈퇴 (Withdraw).
  3. Read the notice and press 탈퇴 요청 (Request withdrawal).

From the request you can no longer log in, and every logged-in device is disconnected.

2. If you cannot run the game

You can request deletion at lwh970924@gmail.com. To confirm your identity, include your character name and your Steam profile URL. The Company handles the request within 7 business days of receipt and tells you the result.

3. Deletion schedule

  1. For 30 days from the request, you can cancel the withdrawal by logging in with the same account.
  2. After 30 days, login data (Steam ID), login records, device and IP records and chat messages are deleted, and character names and similar data are changed so no one can identify you. This cannot be undone.
  3. Records that must be kept by law, such as payment records and currency ledgers, are kept separately for 5 years from the last record and then destroyed.
  4. If a restriction, report or payment is in progress, processing may be deferred for up to 180 days until it is settled.

4. Please note

  1. Remaining Paid Star Shards are forfeited on withdrawal. If you want a refund, request it under the Paid Service Policy before withdrawing.
  2. Withdrawal cannot be requested while a payment is in progress.

Details follow Article 3 of the Privacy Policy.
