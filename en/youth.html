Youth Protection Policy

This Policy is a translation of the Korean original. If the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

네코랩 (nekolab) (the "Company") sets out this Youth Protection Policy so that young people are not exposed to harmful content and can enjoy the game in a healthy way.

Article 1 (Minimum Age)

Only persons aged 14 or older may register for Bolon. If the Company learns that a child under 14 has registered, it stops the account's use and destroys its personal information.

Article 2 (Blocking Harmful Content)

  1. A banned-word filter keeps abuse, sexual and hateful expressions out of chat, character names and party recruitment posts. Banned words in chat are masked; they cannot be used in names at all.
  2. Users can report and block harmful chat or users. Reports are handled under the Operation Policy.
  3. Users who repeatedly post content harmful to young people are sanctioned under the Operation Policy.

Article 3 (Game Rating)

Bolon will apply for a rating from the Game Rating and Administration Committee of Korea. Once rated, the rating and content descriptors will be shown on the start screen, the store page and in this Policy.

Article 4 (Game Time Selection)

  1. A user under 18, or the user's legal guardian, may request that the user's play time be limited.
  2. To request it, send to lwh970924@gmail.com the character name, the desired hours of play, and proof of the requester's relationship to the user.
  3. The Company applies the setting within 7 business days of the request and reports the result. Login is not possible outside the set hours.
  4. A legal guardian may change or remove the setting in the same way.

Article 5 (Preventing Overuse)

  1. Long play sessions can harm health. A break every hour is recommended.
  2. If you are worried about gaming overuse, use Game Time Selection or seek help from counselling services such as the Game Culture Foundation's healing center.

Article 6 (Payments and Guardians' Rights)

If a minor pays without the consent of a legal guardian, the minor or the guardian may cancel the payment. The procedure follows Article 5 of the Paid Service Policy.
